WType 0.2.0 adds paragraph alternatives, a refreshed writing interface, and more control over appearance.

- **Paragraph alternatives:** keep multiple drafts of a passage, choose any version from a menu, and rename or remove drafts with Undo/Redo support. Normal saves and recovery retain every version; PDF and current-version Markdown exports include the chosen text.
- **Refreshed interface:** compact line icons, grouped formatting controls, a cleaner document header, and a dismissible versions bar.
- **Improved Appearance settings:** separate writing and interface font sizes, responsive controls, and document zoom that preserves content and formatting.
- **Smoother sliders:** direct dragging, animated focus feedback, and fewer expensive redraws when adjusting zoom and opacity.
- **Three new light themes:** Catppuccin Latte, Solarized Light, and Gruvbox Light.
- **Consistent PDF fonts:** embedded Outfit body text, Cascadia Mono code, and Vazirmatn for Persian and Arabic.
- **Layout fixes:** prevent status messages and font-size controls from overlapping.
